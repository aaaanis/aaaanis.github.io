Playing Skyio

How do I win?

Keep your score low. Reveal, draw, and swap cards in your twelve-card grid. Three matching revealed cards in a column clear that column. When either player's total reaches 100, the lowest total wins. Open the book button on the home screen for the full rules.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. Solo, local two-player games, and Today's Deal work offline. Your unfinished offline match is saved on your iPhone.

How do I play with a friend?

On one phone, choose Play locally and enter both names. On separate phones, both players need Skyio, an internet connection, and Apple Game Center accounts. Choose Play online to use Game Center matchmaking and invitations.

How do I change the language?

Skyio supports English, French, Spanish, German, and Italian. It uses your iPhone's preferred supported language. You can choose a language for Skyio in your iPhone's app settings.

Ads and purchases

When do ads appear?

A full-screen ad may appear when you choose Replay after a completed match. If an ad isn't available, your next game starts right away.

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings from the gear on the home screen and choose Remove Ads. This is a one-time purchase. The price shown is the App Store price for your region.

I already purchased Remove Ads.

Use the same Apple Account you purchased with, then open Skyio's Settings and choose Restore Purchases. An internet connection may be needed. If you still see ads, email us with your iOS and Skyio version numbers.

Where are my privacy choices?

Open Settings in Skyio. When Google's consent service makes privacy options available, a Privacy choices entry appears there. You can also read our Privacy Policy.
