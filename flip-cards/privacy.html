Flip Cards Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Anis Akeb (“we”) provides Flip Cards, an iPhone card game. This policy explains what the game and its integrated providers (Apple and Google) do with information. We do not run our own servers for the game and we do not create accounts.

Game data on your device

The app stores on your device: your coins, the card backs and tables you own, your statistics, your daily deal streak, your settings, the names you type for pass-and-play, an unfinished offline game so you can resume it, and the list of online players you blocked. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Online play with Game Center

Online play needs an Apple Game Center account and a network connection. Apple handles sign-in, matchmaking and invitations. During an online game, your Game Center display name, your moves, the table, lobby choices and chat messages or reactions are sent to the other players in that game through Game Center's connection, not through our servers. We do not store online games or chat.

Free-text chat is only available in games made from invitations (invite-only). Quick matches with other people allow preset reactions only. The hosting phone filters messages for offensive words before they are shown and limits how often each player can send. You can block a player (their messages and reactions are hidden in every game on your phone) and report a player.

Reporting opens an email to us in your mail app, prefilled with the reported player's display name, Game Center player ID and their recent messages from that game. We receive it only if you send it, and we use it to review abuse reports. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Game Center.

Daily deal reminder

If you turn on the reminder, the app asks for permission and schedules notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to a notification service.

Advertising and consent

The free version shows Google AdMob ads: a banner on the round summary, game-over, statistics, shop and rules screens; a full-screen ad at most every three minutes when you leave a finished game (never during your first session); and optional video ads you choose to watch for a reward (“Save my round”, “Double coins”). Flip Cards requests non-personalized ads, limits ads to general audiences, disables Google's publisher first-party identifier and does not ask to access Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a consent message is required in your region and whether ads can be requested. Where required, Settings includes “Privacy choices”. When ads are active, Google and its partners may process information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device or app identifiers where available, ads shown and interactions with them, and diagnostic data, for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Remove Ads purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase goes through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment; the app only reads the verified purchase to stop banner and full-screen ads and to restore it. We never receive your payment details.

Rating prompt

The app may ask Apple's system to show its rating prompt after a win. Apple controls when it appears and handles any rating or review you leave.

Children

Flip Cards is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices and contact

You can use Privacy choices (when shown), buy Remove Ads, turn off the reminder, block players, or delete the app to remove its local data. Apple and Google handle their data under their own policies. For questions or to exercise the privacy rights available in your region, contact us at anis.akeb@gmail.com.
