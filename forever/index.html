A fan-made companion for WoW: Forever

Forever Talents

Plan your next build. Explore all three talent trees, see how talents affect your spells, save your ideas and share an image or build link with other players.

Support

Need help or found a problem? Email anis.akeb@gmail.com with your app version, device and a description. Please do not include unnecessary personal information.

Your builds

Your builds are saved on your device, and the calculator works offline using the included game data. No account is needed. Share a build as an image or a link that opens its talents directly. Talent data checks for updates when you open the app.

Privacy

Forever Talents is free and supported by ads. Read the privacy policy for information about local saves, sharing, advertising and your choices.
