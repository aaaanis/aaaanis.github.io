Forever Talents

Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Anis Akeb

Forever Talents is a free fan-made talent calculator for World of Warcraft: Forever. This policy describes the information used by the app and its providers.

On your device

Your saved builds, settings, cached talent data and icons, onboarding progress, and counters that limit ads and rating prompts are stored on your device. The app does not require an account or collect a player profile. Saved builds are not uploaded to an app database. Calculating talents and saving builds work offline with the data included in the app.

Game data and network requests

The app checks for updated talent and spell data and loads the talent changelog from public files hosted by Supabase Storage. Game icons are fetched from Blizzard’s CDN. These providers receive normal network request information, such as your IP address, the requested file and technical information about the request. These requests do not include your saved builds. See Supabase’s privacy policy and Blizzard’s privacy policy.

Sharing builds

Sharing is optional. Build links contain the class and selected talents in the link itself, so someone with the link can open that build. Shared images show the build and any build title included in the preview. The app uses your device’s share interface; the destination you choose receives the content you share and handles it under its own policy. Deleting a local build does not remove copies you have already shared.

Shared links can also open a web calculator hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting that website sends normal request information, including your IP address and the requested URL, to its hosting provider. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Advertising and your choices

The native app uses Google AdMob interstitial ads at natural breaks after saving, sharing or leaving a calculator. Ads are spaced apart and do not appear immediately on first use. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to collect consent where required and requests Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS.

Google and its partners may process device or advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address and approximate location, advertising interactions, crash logs and performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising depends on your consent and platform settings. See Google’s privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

When required by Google’s consent system, Settings → Ad privacy options lets you change your choices. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. The app remains usable without allowing tracking.

Ratings and support

The app uses the platform’s own rating interface. Apple or Google processes ratings under its own policies. If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and troubleshoot the problem. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Retention and deletion

You can delete saved builds in My Builds. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Copies exported to Photos, Files or another app remain until you remove them there. Device backups are managed by your platform and its settings.

Support messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. For questions or requests about support correspondence, contact anis.akeb@gmail.com. Advertising and hosting providers may retain their own records under their privacy and retention policies; deleting the app does not automatically delete those records.

Children and contact

Forever Talents is not directed at children under 13. For privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact Anis Akeb at anis.akeb@gmail.com.
