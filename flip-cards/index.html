Flip Cards support

A push-your-luck card game for iPhone, by Anis Akeb.

Questions, purchase help or player reports: anis.akeb@gmail.com.

Playing

Solo, daily deal and pass-and-play work offline. Online play for 2–4 players requires an internet connection and Apple Game Center accounts. Open Online to invite friends or find a quick match. The in-game Rules explain how to play.

Ads and purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase in Settings. It removes banners and full-screen ads, and rewarded video bonuses become available without watching ads. To recover an existing purchase, use the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in Settings.

Online safety

Free-text chat is available only in invite-only games. Quick matches allow preset reactions. You can block players or report them by email from the game. Reports are sent only when you send the prefilled email.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy. Where available, use Privacy choices in Settings to manage advertising consent.

Languages

Flip Cards supports English, French, Spanish, German and Italian, using your iPhone’s preferred supported language.
