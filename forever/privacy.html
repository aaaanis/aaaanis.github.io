Forever Talents

Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026 · Anis Akeb

Forever Talents is a free fan-made talent calculator for World of Warcraft: Forever. This policy describes the information used by the app and its providers.

On your device

Saved builds, settings, cached talent data and icons, sign-in session, onboarding progress, and counters that limit ads and rating prompts are stored on your device. Calculating talents and saving builds locally do not require an account. Removing the app removes its local data; it does not delete published content or an online account.

Online profiles and builds

Online features use Supabase. Choosing a display name, voting, or publishing creates an anonymous account. You may add an email address for sign-in and recovery on another device. We store your account identifier, display name, optional email address, published builds, votes and their timestamps. Published builds and display names are public. Email addresses and individual voting records are not presented publicly. Aggregated talent pick rates are calculated from published builds.

Supabase processes network and technical information, including IP addresses, to provide authentication, storage, security and abuse prevention. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

Reports, blocks and community safety

When you report a build or a player, we store your account identifier, the reported build or player identifier, the reason, any optional note, the report time and its review status. We use these records to review reports, prevent abuse and enforce the community rules. The app does not show your reporter identity to the reported player. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information in your note.

Report summaries are also sent to the developer through issues in a private GitHub repository and its email notifications. These summaries include the reported build or player name, reason, time and an excerpt of the optional note, but not the reporter’s account identifier. GitHub processes these alerts under its privacy statement.

When you block a player, we store your account identifier, the blocked player’s identifier and the block time. This hides their builds from your leaderboard. Your block list is not shown to other players, and you can remove blocks from the Account tab. Publishing requires accepting the community rules shown in the app; your acceptance is stored on your device.

Advertising

The native app uses Google AdMob interstitial ads at natural breaks after saving, sharing, publishing or leaving a calculator. Ads are spaced apart and do not appear immediately on first use. The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to collect consent where required, and requests Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS. Google and its partners may process device or advertising identifiers where permitted, IP address and approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising depends on your consent and platform settings. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

When required by Google’s consent system, the Account tab includes “Ad privacy options” to change your choices. You can also manage tracking permission in iOS Settings. The app remains usable without allowing tracking.

Game content, sharing and ratings

Game icons are fetched from Blizzard’s CDN, which receives normal network request information such as your IP address. Shared build links or images contain the build information and may include your chosen display name; the destination you choose receives that content. The app uses the platform’s own rating interface. Apple or Google processes ratings under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Online profile information, published builds, votes, reports and blocks remain until removed or no longer needed for the service. You can delete your own published builds and unblock players in the app.

To delete your online account, open Account → Delete my account and confirm. When the request succeeds, your account, profile, display name, optional sign-in email, published builds, votes, and associated report and block records are deleted from the active app database. Builds saved only on your device remain. Removing the app alone does not delete your online account.

Account deletion does not automatically erase report summaries already sent to the developer’s private GitHub issues or email notifications. For deletion of these copies, account deletion if you cannot access the app, or another privacy request, contact anis.akeb@gmail.com; we may need to verify account ownership before acting. Hosting providers may retain backups or security logs under their own retention policies.

Children and contact

Forever Talents is not directed at children under 13. For questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact Anis Akeb at anis.akeb@gmail.com.
