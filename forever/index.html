A fan-made companion for WoW: Forever

Forever Talents

Plan your next build. Explore all three talent trees, see how talents affect your spells, save your ideas and share them with other players.

Support

Need help or found a problem? Email anis.akeb@gmail.com with your app version, device and a description. Please do not include passwords.

Your builds

The calculator and local saves work without signing in. An optional email lets you access your online profile on another device. Talent data checks for updates when you open the app.
