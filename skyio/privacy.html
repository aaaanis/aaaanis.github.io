Skyio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Anis Akeb (“we”) provides Skyio, an iPhone card game. This policy explains how the game and its integrated providers handle information.

Game data on your device

The app stores your current offline match on your device so you can resume it. That save contains card and turn state, scores, and any player names you enter. The app also stores whether you started today's deal on that date. We do not receive this local game data. Leaving a match clears its save; deleting the app removes its local data.

Game Center online matches

Online 1v1 requires an Apple Game Center account and a network connection. Apple's Game Center handles player authentication and matchmaking. During a live match, your Game Center display name, moves, revealed cards, and scores are sent to your opponent through the match connection. The game host keeps the shuffled deck and sends snapshots with covered card values hidden. Online matches are not saved for later resumption. See Apple's Privacy Policy for Apple's handling of Game Center information.

Advertising and consent

The free version may show a Google AdMob full-screen ad after a completed match when you choose Replay. Each player in an online match may see an ad on their own device. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a privacy message is required and whether ads can be requested. Where UMP requires it, Settings includes “Privacy choices” so you can revisit available choices. If UMP does not permit ad requests, the app does not request ads.

When Google advertising services are active, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device or app identifiers where available, ads shown and interactions with them, app interaction data, and diagnostic or performance data. The purposes and parties depend on the region, your choices, and the advertising configuration. See Google's Privacy Policy and the choices shown in the UMP message for more information.

Remove Ads purchase

You can buy the one-time Remove Ads product through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple handles payment and purchase records; Skyio reads the product details and verified purchase entitlement to stop showing full-screen ads and to restore your purchase. We do not receive your payment card details. On a later launch with an active Remove Ads entitlement, the app skips ad setup. If you buy during a session in which the advertising SDK was already started, it may have processed data before the purchase.

Your choices and retention

You can stop a match to clear its save, remove the app to clear its local data, use the in-app Privacy choices entry when available, or buy Remove Ads to stop full-screen ads. Google and Apple manage information they process under their own policies and retention practices; see Google and Apple. If you have a question about this app's handling of data or wish to exercise privacy rights available in your region, contact us at anis.akeb@gmail.com.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. The effective date above will show the latest version.
